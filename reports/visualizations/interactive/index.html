What these plots suggest

Sample structure (PCA)

The submitted static PCA shows the three young PDL20 samples grouped closely together, while the four late/RS samples are displaced from them. This is consistent with a substantial transcriptome difference between young and late samples. The RS samples also split into a PDL46/TP6 pair and a PDL50/TP7 pair, suggesting appreciable heterogeneity within the late samples. Because passage/timepoint and condition may be entangled, this split should not automatically be called a biological subtype or a technical batch effect. PC1 explained about 72.9% and PC2 about 11.3% in the original static plot; regenerated values are shown in the interactive PCA axes.

Expression heatmap

The heatmap displays row Z-scores, not absolute expression: colors indicate each gene's relative expression across the seven samples. The broad young-versus-late pattern supports group-level differences among the selected genes, while sample-specific bright cells indicate genes that vary particularly strongly in one replicate. Hover a cell to see its gene, sample, TPM, ground-truth log2 fold change, and row Z-score. The top 100 usable rows were plotted; 100 of the 100 selected ground-truth genes matched to the TPM table.

Volcano plot

The volcano plot uses the previously generated exploratory limma results from log2(TPM + 0.001). In that model, positive log2 fold change indicates higher expression in RS and negative log2 fold change indicates lower expression in RS. Top genes classified as Up in RS by adjusted p-value < 0.05 and log2FC ≥ 1: FOXE1, PTCSC2, DAPK1, MEGF6, CD4, F3, GPRC5A, C1orf115. Top genes classified as Down in RS by adjusted p-value < 0.05 and log2FC ≤ −1: COLEC12, VGF, EGR1, NDN, SPHK1, THNSL2, IRF6, ARMCX1. The adjusted-p-value-ranked genes in the input results include: FOXE1, PTCSC2, DAPK1, MEGF6, CD4, COLEC12, F3, GPRC5A, C1orf115, VGF, RAI2, GFRA1. Inspect all genes through hover rather than relying only on these top names. Very large fold changes can arise when expression is near zero in one group, so check per-sample TPM before biological interpretation.

In the generated table, 5657 genes have adjusted p-value < 0.05, including 832 meeting the additional exploratory threshold log2FC ≥ 1 and 1032 meeting log2FC ≤ −1. These are summaries of the TPM-based limma model and are not definitive count-based differential-expression results.

Scientific caveat

Exploratory only. TPM values are not raw RNA-seq counts. The p-values in the volcano plot should not be presented as definitive differential-expression evidence. For formal inference, use the appropriate raw counts or estimated counts with a method such as DESeq2, edgeR, or limma-voom, and verify the sample design (including passage/timepoint and any batch variables).
